# Research scope and acceptance boundaries

This project is an independent concept implementation. It does not assert a United
Therapeutics relationship or reproduce any company's data, protocols or internal systems.
The supplied example is entirely fictional, including its model, endpoint and units.

Evidence integrity is not evidence validity. Matching bytes only confirms the supplied
digest. Context and source fields are caller declarations, and exact matching does not
establish the underlying organism, experimental model or endpoint is correctly described.
An expiry rule is supplied policy, not a conclusion about scientific shelf life.

The assay summary is elementary descriptive arithmetic on a supplied table. Replicate
IDs need not be statistically independent; the tool does not identify experimental
units, randomization, batch effects, missingness or confounding. A difference between
two means is not a causal treatment effect, efficacy result or safety finding.
No confidence interval, hypothesis test or clinical interpretation is produced.

Review bindings prevent accidental reuse after declared claim/evidence changes. They
do not prove that a reviewer read the evidence, has expertise, is independent or is
the named person. Source/reviewer authentication, provenance custody and conflicts
of interest require a separately designed and evaluated process.

The ResearchOS adapter supports a bounded claim about local checks on supplied data.
It does not certify the scientific statement text. Domain attack and independent
reproduction gates remain open even when every local dossier check passes. Local
regression tests are software checks and cannot close those external gates.

Before any real research study, define the intended question and model, document
data permissions and provenance, version protocols/analysis, choose an independently
reviewed statistical design, preserve original observations and audit negative results.
Real clinical deployment or company integration is outside this implementation and
requires its own independently authorized project scope. No accounts, services,
messages, patient workflows, equipment actions or scheduled activity are enabled.
